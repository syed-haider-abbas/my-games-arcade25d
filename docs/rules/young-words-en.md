# Young Words

## Objective

Build words and sentences to help storybook characters complete their adventures.

## How to Play

* Choose a language, then choose a chapter.
* In the chapter, choose a learning level first. Then choose the next story part that is ready. You can play an earlier part again; locked parts will open later.
* Read what is happening in the story, then read the question.
* Tap or move the word pieces to build the sentence.
* See what your answer changes in the story.
* Ask for help when you need it. Mistakes are part of learning.
* First notice how the words work, then try with help, and finally take your own turn.

## Pro Tip

Look at who is acting, what they are doing, and when or where it happens.
