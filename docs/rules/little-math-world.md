# Little Math World

## Your objective

Choose your explorer and follow the Grade 2 adventure path. Each place teaches a new idea, and each stage gives you a chance to show what you understand.

## How to play

* **Look at the math.** See the blocks, numbers, or shapes.
* **Work on one problem.** Tap, move, match, choose, or build.
* **Ask for a hint.** A hint shows you what to try.
* **Try again when something feels tricky.** A mistake is a clue about what to notice next.
* **Master the stage.** Solve varied problems on your own to open the next path.
* **Collect discoveries.** Badges and journey decorations celebrate learning; there are no scores or timers.

## Your journey

Little Math World is arranged like this:

**Grade 2 → Adventure Chapter → Concept → Stage → Problems**

Completed stages can always be replayed with fresh challenges. Your progress belongs to your profile and stays on this device.

## Pro Tip

Look at the picture, say the idea out loud, and explain why your answer works. Careful thinking helps your number lanterns shine!
